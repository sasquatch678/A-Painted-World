## <p align="center">CRAFTING</p>

A wide variety of useful items can be crafted using different tools and crafting stations found throughout Cyrodiil. Crafting experience is awarded based on the material cost of the item being created.

Many crafting stations are already integrated into the world. Wells, kegs, tanning racks, cooking pots, and anvils are automatically converted into usable crafting objects wherever they are found.

Additional stations have been placed throughout the world, including:

* **Smelters and workbenches** in every Blacksmith shop
* **Tanning racks** in every Fighters Guildhall
* **Sewing stations** in every Mages Guildhall

All of these stations can also be purchased from miscellaneous vendors or crafted into placeable objects, allowing you to set up crafting stations in your own homes.

There are also smaller crafting tools such as **shears and pickaxes**. These are used to harvest specific resources that can then be processed at the larger crafting stations.

## <p align="center">RESOURCE GATHERING</p>

Resources can be gathered directly from the world and processed into materials used throughout the crafting system.

### Mining

Any rock in the world can be mined using a **pickaxe**. After several swings, the rock will drop a piece of stone onto the ground.

Stone can be processed at a **workbench** and is used in a variety of crafting recipes.

Rocks found inside dungeons can also randomly become **ore veins**. Mining these veins produces ore that can be smelted into ingots.

### Woodcutting

Any tree in the world can be chopped using an **axe**. After several swings, a log will drop onto the ground.

Logs can be processed into **boards** at a workbench, which are used in many crafting recipes.

### Water

Wells throughout Cyrodiil can be used to collect water. Simply interact with a well while carrying an **empty bucket**, and it will be filled with water.

Water is used in several crafting recipes.

### Milk

Cows can be milked by interacting with them while carrying a **pitcher or empty bucket**.

The resulting milk can be **pasteurized at a cooking pot** and is used in several crafting recipes.

### Ore & Ingots

Ore veins found throughout dungeons can be mined and processed into **ingots at a smelter**.

Ingots are primarily used to craft weapons and armor at an anvil, but are also required for various other recipes.

Ingots can occasionally be found as random loot or purchased from merchants.

### Wool & Cloth

Sheep can be sheared by interacting with them while carrying **shears**. This harvests wool, which can be processed into **yarn at a spinning wheel**.

Yarn can then be woven into **cloth**, which is used to create a wide variety of clothing and other textile-based items.

## <p align="center">FISHING</p>

Fishing provides another way to gather resources and make some extra coin.

To get started, visit **The Flowing Bowl in Anvil** and speak with **Maenlorn**. He sells several types of fishing rods and the guidebook *All About Fishing*, and can provide additional information about the fishing system.

The standard wooden fishing rod can also be purchased from:

* **Imperial City:** Jensine or Sergius from Three Brothers
* **Bravil:** The Fair Deal
* **Leyawiin:** Best Goods and Guarantees

### How to Fish

Equip a fishing rod and swing while facing a body of water. You will see a small splash when your line lands and your bobber is in place.

When you see a **large splash**, quickly reel in your line by **blocking (Right Click)** to catch the fish.

There are many different species of fish to discover, including some extremely rare and valuable catches. Fishing can be a worthwhile source of income, and you may also occasionally fish up miscellaneous items.
