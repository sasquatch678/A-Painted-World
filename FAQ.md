### There are a lot of disabled plugins. Should I enable them?

No. These plugins are already included in a merged plugin to bypass the 255-plugin limit. Do not enable them or make any changes to them.

### I am getting an application load error 5:0000065434 when trying to launch the game.

Launch Steam before starting the game.

### My mouse isn't working

Open `oblivion.ini` in the `profiles` folder where you installed the modlist and change `bBackground Mouse` to `1`.

### The game crashes on launch

Try disabling DXVK in MO2. If this resolves the crash, update your graphics drivers and re-enable DXVK. If the game still crashes, try a different version of DXVK or play without it.

### How do I take screenshots?

Press the Print Screen key on the numpad. Screenshots are saved to the `overwrite/Root` folder.

You can change the screenshot key and save location through the ReShade UI, which can be opened by pressing `Home`.

### How do I start the main quest?

Commit a crime, [or become falsely accused of one](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Imperial_Corruption), and get arrested by an Imperial Legion guard. You will begin the tutorial in your prison cell.

After completing the sewer sequence, you can retrieve your belongings from the evidence chest back in the Prison District.

### I died in a location I can't get back to.

Due to the nature of certain quests, this can happen. You can use the console command `player.moveto migDeadPlayer` to teleport to your corpse.

Alternatively, close the game and reload your last save as normal.
